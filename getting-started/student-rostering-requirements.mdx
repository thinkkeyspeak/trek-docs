To roster students in Trek, your school must provide **student rosters** and **student email addresses**.

## How rostering works

Schools can:

- **Manage rosters directly** in Trek, or
- **Send roster files** to the Trek team for processing and upload

Work with your administrator to confirm which method your district uses before students need access.
