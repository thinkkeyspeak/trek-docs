Trek is a **fully web-based platform**. No software installation is required on student or teacher devices.

## Supported browsers

Trek works on all modern browsers, including:

- **Google Chrome**
- **Safari**
- **Mozilla Firefox**

Use the latest browser version your district supports for the best experience.

<Note>
  If students cannot load Trek, check that your school network allows access to Trek domains and that pop-up or content filters are not blocking the site.
</Note>
