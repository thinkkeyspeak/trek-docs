Trek supports two methods for integrating with school systems.

## Integration options

| Method | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **EdLink** | Most student information systems (SIS). Preferred and most common among Trek partner schools. |
| **Bulk CSV upload** | Districts that export rosters from their SIS and upload files to Trek |

## EdLink (recommended)

EdLink works with most SIS platforms. Contact your administrator or the Trek team to enable EdLink for your school.

## CSV roster upload

If your school uses CSV upload, coordinate with your admin on file format and upload schedule so rosters stay current.
