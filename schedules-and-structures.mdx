Rolling out TrekAi well often means thinking beyond login day — how it fits into your daily and weekly schedule, and how expectations shift across grade levels.

We're building dedicated guidance on **scheduling TrekAi successfully across grade levels**, along with subject-specific guides for **using TrekAi in reading** and **using TrekAi in math**, so teachers have concrete starting points rather than a blank slate.

_This resource is in development and will be added to the Map the Expedition section of our support site soon. In the meantime, your Partner Success Manager can help you think through scheduling and grade-level rollout for your school._
