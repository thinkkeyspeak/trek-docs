The TrekAi Student Certification, _Thinking Critically and Responsibly with AI_, gives students the AI literacy skills they need for both the classroom and everyday life. Through an interactive, self-paced course, students learn what AI is (and isn't), explore digital safety and academic integrity, and practice using AI as a revision and feedback tool without compromising honest work. Students also build the critical thinking skills to spot misinformation and bias in AI output walking away certified as responsible, forward-thinking digital citizens.

## Student Tools:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Core Learning Tools">
    - **Proofread** – Reviews written work and provides feedback on grammar, clarity, and structure.
    - **Tutor** – Offers personalized, on-demand help by answering questions and guiding students through problem-solving.
    - **Quiz** – Generates customized practice quizzes on any topic to reinforce learning.
    - **AI Chat** – Real-time, open-ended conversation for exploring ideas or working through schoolwork.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Assignments & Progress">
    - **All Assignments** – View and complete tasks shared by teachers.
    - **Chat History** – Revisit past conversations to track learning progress.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Uploads & Materials">
    - **My Uploads** – Access previously uploaded files for reuse in activities.
    - **My Sections** – View current course enrollments connected to TrekAi.
    - **Upload Image / Upload Document** – Add materials to power personalized tutoring, proofreading, or study activities.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Personalization">
    - **TrekAi Personality** – Lets students choose a tone/style that makes learning more engaging.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Specialized Tools">
    - **College Essay Brainstormer** – Helps generate and refine ideas for college application essays through guided questions and feedback.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
