---
title: "Customizing Your TrekAi"
---

## **School-Level Customizations**

- **Curriculum Alignment** — TrekAi is trained on your school's specific course enrollments and curriculum.

- **Feature Toggles** — Tools can be turned on/off at the school level.
- **District-Wide Policies** — Admins set overarching policies that apply across the school.
- **Data Housing** — All data lives within the school's own secure tenancy.

## **Teacher-Level Customizations**

- **Custom Activity Tool** — Teachers can customize how Trek behaves to match their teaching style, lesson goals, or student needs.
- **TrekAi Personalities** — Students select a tone/style, often set up by the teacher's classroom preferences.
- **Custom Rubrics & Grading Style** — Teachers upload their own rubrics and "train" Trek to grade the way they would.
- **Content Uploads** — Teachers upload their own documents, study guides, and materials to personalize activities for their class.

## Student-Level Customizations

- **TrekAi Personality** — Students can choose from different personalities, each with its own tone and style, to make interactions feel more engaging.
- **Content Uploads** — Students can upload their own documents and images to personalize tutoring, proofreading, or study activities.
- **Accessibility Preferences** — Text-to-speech and speech-to-text support students who need it.
- **Language Support** — Fluent multilingual support lets students engage with TrekAi in their preferred language.
- **Focused Study Materials** — Students can direct TrekAi to focus specifically on teacher-uploaded content (like a textbook, PowerPoint, or review guide) rather than general knowledge.
- **Chat History Review** — Students can revisit and reference their own past conversations to support ongoing learning.
