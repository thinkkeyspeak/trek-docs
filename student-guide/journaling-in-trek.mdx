Journaling in Trek is a **reflection tool** designed primarily for elementary students.

## How it works

1. The teacher provides a **prompt**.
2. Students respond by expanding on ideas, reflecting on what they learned, and practicing expressive writing.

Journaling is not a general chat—it is structured around teacher-led prompts so students build writing skills in a supported environment.
