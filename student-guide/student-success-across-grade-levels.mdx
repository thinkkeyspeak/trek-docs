Students at every grade level can use Trek for instructional support, practice, and feedback—the platform is not limited to one age group.

## Who adapts fastest

Middle school and high school students often pick up Trek quickly because they are already comfortable with digital tools for schoolwork.

## All grade levels benefit

Younger students gain from guided activities (such as [journaling](/student-guide/journaling-in-trek)) and accessibility features. Older students use Trek for research, writing, revision, and test preparation.
