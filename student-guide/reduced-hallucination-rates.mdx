General AI chat tools can invent facts because they search the open web. Trek is built differently for classrooms.

## Why Trek is more reliable

Trek relies on a **curated knowledge base** rather than open-web retrieval. That design:

- Reduces misinformation
- Keeps hallucination rates **below 2%** in typical use
- Improves reliability for homework, research, and study
