Trek is built for learners across elementary through high school, with tools that adapt to different ages and needs.

## Typical grade ranges

- **Most common:** Grades 6–12 (middle and high school)
- **Also supported:** Some schools use Trek with students as early as **grade 3**

## Accessibility

Trek includes accessibility features such as **text-to-speech**, which helps younger learners and students with diverse learning needs access content more easily.
