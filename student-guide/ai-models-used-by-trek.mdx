Trek uses a variety of large language models—including Claude, OpenAI models, and GPT-4o—to match the right model to each task.

## Your data stays protected

- Student and school data are **stored securely**
- Data is **not shared with third parties** for their own use
- Trek does **not** use your school's data to train external AI models
