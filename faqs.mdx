  <Accordion title="General">
    **Q: What is TrekAi?** A: TrekAi is an AI-powered learning platform designed specifically for K–12 schools. It provides students and teachers with a safe, structured environment to use artificial intelligence for learning, critical thinking, writing support, project-based learning, and skill development. TrekAi is built with school-friendly safeguards, administrative controls, and curriculum alignment to support classroom instruction—not replace it.

    **Q: What problems does TrekAi solve for schools?** A: TrekAi helps schools address several key challenges:

    - **Safe AI Access:** Provides students with a monitored, education-focused AI platform instead of unrestricted public AI tools.
    - **Teacher Efficiency:** Supports teachers with instructional resources, feedback tools, and project-based learning ideas to reduce prep time.
    - **Student Skill Gaps:** Helps students strengthen writing, analysis, research, and critical thinking skills through guided AI interaction.
    - **Consistency & Oversight:** Gives administrators visibility into usage, ensuring AI is used responsibly and aligns with school policies.
    - **Equitable Access:** Ensures all students have structured access to AI tools, rather than only those who find external tools on their own.

    **Q: Is TrekAi available internationally?** A: Yes, we have teachers and students using the platform globally and work with partner schools from various countries.

    **Q: Who do I contact for technical support?** A: We are here to help! For assistance, please email our support team at [help@trekai.org](mailto:help@trekai.org).

    **Q: I'd like to explore TrekAi before enrolling. Can I try TrekAi before enrolling?** A: We'd be happy to show you how TrekAi works. Please schedule a demo with our Sales Team at [trekai.org/demo](https://trekai.org/demo).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Account & Login">
    **Q: Is there a way to prompt the system to resend the login email?** A: Yes! As a school admin, you can resend login emails. Navigate to Users \> Pencil icon next to the student's name \> Reset password.

    **Q: Does TrekAi support single sign-on?** A: Yes! We have both Google and Microsoft Single Sign-On. No matter what LMS/SIS you sync with, you will be able to SSO with Google or Microsoft.

    **Q: Is my data secure?** A: Absolutely! We're COPPA and FERPA compliant. Everything you put into Trek is yours and not ours. We do not use it to train any other models and any agreements we have with providers say they will not use it to train their models. We don't sell data and we don't use anything you upload to train other models. We're pretty tight on all data privacy.

    **Q: How can I increase the number of user accounts (licenses) for my school?** A: To request additional user accounts or licenses, please contact your Partner Success Manager. They'll guide you through the process and help adjust your plan based on your school or district's needs.

    **Q: What roles are available in my TrekAi account?** A: TrekAi offers three primary user roles: School Admin, Teacher, and Student. Each role includes specific permissions and access levels to ensure the platform is used effectively and securely within your school or district.

    **Q: How do we add or remove admins?** A: Users with School Admin access can manage admin permissions in the Admin Dashboard. To update a user's role, navigate to Admin in the left-hand menu, select All Users, then click Edit User next to the appropriate name. From there, update the user's role under User Role and save your changes.

    **Q: Our students don't have email addresses. Can they still get TrekAi accounts?** A: Inform your Partner Success Specialist, and they will provide an alternative login solution so your students can access TrekAi.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Student Onboarding & Best Practices">
    **Q: What are the best practices for rolling out TrekAi to students?** A: We recommend a Launch Day, where all students receive an email to create their accounts. A good strategy is to introduce TrekAi in a common class, allowing students to log in, explore, and ask questions before discussing appropriate use. Teachers assigning work through TrekAi also helps with adoption.

    **Q: What are the best practices for using TrekAi in math?** A: Math teachers use TrekAi as a homework companion or a flipped classroom tool. Recommended prompts include:

    - "Ask Trek to teach you about right triangles."
    - "Ask Trek to explain the Pythagorean theorem."
    - "Ask Trek to quiz you on the Pythagorean theorem."

    Students can also generate practice problems for self-quizzing.

    **Q: What grades is TrekAi geared towards?** A: TrekAi is most commonly used for grades 6-12, but some schools use it as early as 3rd grade. It also includes text-to-speech for accessibility.

    **Q: Which grades have the most success with TrekAi?** A: Middle and High school students adapt quickly, but the platform benefits all grade levels.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Features & Functionality">
    **Q: What are the differences between the three types of proofreading options?** A: The proofreading options are:

    - **Detailed:** Analyzes the rubric line by line.
    - **Holistic:** Provides a summary-style overview.
    - **Elementary:** A simpler, kid-friendly approach.

    **Q: What type of feedback does TrekAi provide for student-uploaded papers?** A: The student version provides detailed feedback.

    **Q: What is journaling in TrekAi?** A: Journaling is a reflection tool, primarily designed for elementary students. It encourages students to expand on topics based on teacher prompts.

    **Q: What is the daily chat limit for TrekAi?** A: The default cap is 100\+ chats per day, but it can be increased if needed.

    **Q: Can TrekAi detect AI-generated content?** A: No, we do not currently support AI detection due to high false positive rates. However, TrekAi offers tools to design AI-resistant assignments and allows teachers to review all student chats for academic integrity.

    **Q: If someone accidentally inputs sensitive data into Trek, is there a way to erase portions/logs if needed?** A: Yes, we can erase sensitive data if needed. While we do not have an automated detection system for sensitive information, we provide warnings advising against entering personal data. Additionally, TrekAi operates within a secure environment where only designated teachers and admins have access. No data is shared or sold to third parties. We're currently working with Common Sense Privacy to evaluate our practices and initial results put us in the mid 90's on the scorecard, which would be in the top 1% in our category.

    **Q: I'm hesitant about my students using AI. I want them to think for themselves.** A: TrekAi is designed to support, not replace student thinking. Instead of giving direct answers, it uses a Socratic-style approach that guides students with questions and prompts, helping them work through concepts independently and strengthen their critical thinking skills.

    **Q: Will TrekAi support my ELL students?** A: TrekAi is designed to support diverse learners, including English Language Learners (ELLs). The platform provides guided instruction, scaffolded prompts, and adaptive support to help students better understand content and build language skills alongside subject mastery.

    **Q: What triggers a social-emotional alert?** A: A social-emotional alert is triggered when a student message contains language that may indicate emotional distress, safety concerns, or wellbeing risks such as regarding self-harm, violence, weapons, suicide, abuse, or other self-harming activities.

    **Q: How will I be notified about a social emotional alert?** A: When a student's chat triggers a social emotional alert, everyone designated as a social emotional recipient will receive an email notification.

    **Q: Can students see that an alert was triggered?** A: TrekAi will redirect the student to a trusted adult. Alerts are only sent to designated social-emotional recipients. They can then determine the appropriate next steps.

    **Q: Are there guardrails to prevent inappropriate content?** A: TrekAi includes built-in guardrails to filter sensitive or inappropriate topics. If any concerns arise, social alerts are automatically sent to designated social-emotional support recipients for follow-up.

    **Q: How can a teacher access student chat histories?** A: Teachers can view student chat histories by navigating to Chat History \> Student Chats. From there, they can search for individual 1:1 conversations or use filters such as date, class, and other available criteria to find specific chats.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Integration & Data Management">
    **Q: How does TrekAi integrate with school systems?** A: TrekAi supports two integration methods:

    - Bulk export to CSV
    - EdLink integration, which works with most LMS/SIS platforms. This is the most preferred option for our partner schools. Check if your LMS/SIS is compatible [here](https://ed.link/resources/integrations).

    **Q: How does Trek acquire student data (grade level, reading level, etc.)?** A: We import whatever data is available from the LMS we sync with. Reading level is initially inputted in TrekAdventure by the teacher, and from there the system dynamically adjusts based on each student's interactions and progress over time.

    **Q: Can I control what data is shared through EdLink?** A: Yes. Districts can filter their data and choose exactly what is shared with TrekAi. You can learn how to set up sharing rules [here](https://ed.link/docs/platform/sharing/sharing-rules).

    **Q: What student data is required for rostering?** A: Rosters and student email addresses are required. Schools can manage their own rosters or send them to us for processing.

    **Q: Can features be turned on/off for students?** A: Yes, our team can enable or disable tools for specific students.

    **Q: What are the tech requirements for TrekAi?** A: TrekAi is web-based and works on all modern browsers (Safari, Firefox, Chrome).

    **Q: Why is my data incorrect in TrekAi?** A: If your school uses an EdLink integration, your LMS/SIS serves as the source of truth. Any missing or incorrect information in your LMS/SIS will automatically be reflected in TrekAi. Please update the information in your LMS or SIS first, and the changes should sync accordingly.

    Only active students are provisioned. Archived courses will not sync.

    If your account is managed manually, please contact us at [help@trekai.org](mailto:help@trekai.org), and our team will assist with updating your information.

    **Q: How often does TrekAi sync with my LMS/SIS?** A: EdLink syncs automatically every few hours, depending on the LMS or SIS. If you need a manual resync, please contact [EdLink Support](https://ed.link/support). If the issue is on the TrekAi side, reach out to [help@trekai.org](mailto:help@trekai.org).

    **Q: Can I EdLink to more than one LMS/SIS system?** A: TrekAi supports only one active EdLink integration at a time. If you need to switch to a different LMS or SIS, please reach out to [help@trekai.org](mailto:help@trekai.org) for assistance with updating your integration.

    **Q: How long does the onboarding process take?** A: The onboarding timeline depends on your method of data integration. If your LMS or SIS data is ready and properly configured, onboarding can be completed quickly. Manual data uploads may require additional time.

    **Q: How long is data stored? Does it roll over year to year?** A: Data is stored for as long as the account is active, and yes — it absolutely carries over from year to year and moves with the student!
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Content & AI Model">
    **Q: What LLM does TrekAi use?** A: We use a variety of LLMs, including Claude, OpenAI, and GPT-4o, selecting the best model for each task. All data is stored in-house and not shared with third parties.

    **Q: Where does TrekAi pull its information from?** A: TrekAi primarily uses accredited course content (115\+ courses) and a general LLM knowledge base.

    **Q: Why is TrekAi's hallucination rate lower than ChatGPT?** A: Unlike ChatGPT, which pulls from the web, TrekAi is tied to a curated knowledge base, reducing misinformation to below 2%.

    **Q: How is TrekAi different from other AI platforms?** A: TrekAi is a purpose-built educational AI that combines curriculum-aligned tutoring, teacher tools, enhanced accuracy, safety features, and analytics — which sets it apart from general-purpose AI platforms.

    **Q: Does Trek offer any support for checking for plagiarism?** A: The reality is that no AI tool out there can say with complete certainty whether something was written by AI or plagiarized. Even the major detection platforms deal with accuracy issues and false positives. Because of that, we don't rely on a simple "AI or not" score.

    Instead, Trek focuses on giving teachers visibility into student growth over time — including writing patterns, skill development, and overall performance. That way, teachers can see how a student typically writes, how their thinking evolves, and whether a specific submission actually matches their demonstrated ability.

    So if a student uploads something created outside of Trek, teachers still have important context — not just the final submission, but a clear picture of that student's past work and progression.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Teacher Tools & Enhancements">
    **Q: What training is provided?** A: Our Partner Success Team offers both live training sessions and on-demand, pre-recorded trainings. You can choose the format that best fits your teachers' schedules and learning preferences.

    **Q: Does TrekAi offer tools for differentiated instruction?** A: Yes! Teachers can use:

    - **Essential Question Generator** for higher-order thinking.
    - **Lesson Plan Customization** to tailor instruction.
    - **Quiz Generator** with Depth of Knowledge (DOK) levels.
    - **AI-Resistant Assignments** to foster deeper thinking.

    **Q: Is there a tool for project-based learning (PBL)?** A: Yes! The PBL Tool generates project ideas, refines them, and creates lesson plans/rubrics for multidisciplinary learning.

    **Q: Are there tools for AP test preparation?** A: While TrekAi does not have AP-specific content, it supports AP students with:

    - **Quiz Generator** (AP-style questions)
    - **Essential Question Generator** (higher-order analysis)
    - **Tutor Mode** (study guide support)

    **Q: Can I upload my own materials?** A: Teachers can upload their own documents into TrekAi. The platform allows educators to upload files like lesson materials, worksheets, readings, or PDFs so TrekAi can reference that content when generating responses or helping learners.
