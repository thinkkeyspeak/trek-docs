---
title: "Technical Requirements"
---

Good news - there's nothing to install! TrekAi is a fully web-based platform that does not require any software installation and works on all modern browsers, including Chrome, Safari, and Firefox. That means it runs on Chromebooks, laptops, and tablets alike, without IT needing to push out an app or manage updates. 

For rostering, schools connect in one of two ways: a bulk CSV export, or an EdLink integration that works with most student information systems (SIS), with EdLink being the preferred and most commonly used option among TrekAi partner schools.
