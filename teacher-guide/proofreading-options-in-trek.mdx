Trek offers **three proofreading options** to support student writing at different levels.

## Proofreading modes

| Mode | What students get |
| --- | --- |
| **Detailed** | Line-by-line analysis using a rubric |
| **Holistic** | A high-level summary of strengths and areas to improve |
| **Elementary** | Simplified, student-friendly feedback for younger learners |

Choose the mode that matches your grade level and assignment goals. Younger students often benefit from **Elementary**; longer drafts may need **Detailed** feedback.
