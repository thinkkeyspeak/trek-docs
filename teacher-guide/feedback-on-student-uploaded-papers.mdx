When students upload written work into Trek, the **student version** of the platform provides detailed feedback to support revision, clarity, and growth—not just a final grade.

## What students get

Feedback focuses on:

- **Revision** — What to change and why
- **Clarity** — Organization and readability
- **Academic growth** — Actionable next steps

## For teachers

You can still review student work and chats for instructional oversight. Combine Trek's automated feedback with your rubric and class expectations for the best results.
