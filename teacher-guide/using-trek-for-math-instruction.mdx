Math teachers often use Trek as a **homework support tool** or within a **flipped classroom** model.

## Common student uses

Students can ask Trek to:

- **Explain concepts** — For example, right triangles or the Pythagorean theorem
- **Quiz themselves** on a specific topic
- **Generate practice problems** for self-guided study

Set clear expectations in class about when Trek is allowed for math work versus when students must show their own reasoning on paper.
