A successful Trek rollout usually starts with a **planned Launch Day** and clear expectations—not a surprise login on a high-stakes due date.

## Recommended rollout

1. **Launch Day** — Students receive login emails and create accounts.
2. **Introduce Trek together** — Use a common class period so students can explore, ask questions, and learn appropriate use.
3. **Assign work in Trek early** — Short, low-stakes tasks in the first week reinforce adoption and normal usage.

## Before Launch Day

- Confirm [student rostering](/getting-started/student-rostering-requirements) is complete.
- Verify [technical requirements](/getting-started/technical-requirements) for student devices.
- Know how to [resend login emails](/account/resend-login-emails) if someone cannot sign in.
