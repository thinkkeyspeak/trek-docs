---

Getting your school on board with TrekAi starts with trust. Rather than relying on AI-detection tools (which carry high false-positive rates), TrekAi supports academic integrity through AI-resistant assignment design and full teacher visibility into student chats — so you can see exactly how a student worked through a problem. Add in clear answers on data privacy (FERPA/COPPA compliant, student data never sold or used to train outside models), and most hesitations start to ease. 

Once you're up and running, ongoing support helps keep momentum going:

- **Webinars** throughout the year on getting started, advanced features, and refreshing usage mid-year
- **Fun engagement pushes** to build everyday habits with TrekAi, not just one-time logins
- **Help spotting "champions"** — staff who can model and advocate for TrekAi with peers
- **Regular check-ins** with your Partner Success Manager to revisit goals and troubleshoot
