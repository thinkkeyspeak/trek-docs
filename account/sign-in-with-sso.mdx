Trek supports Single Sign-On (SSO) so students and staff can sign in with credentials they already use at school.

## Supported providers

Trek currently integrates with:

- **Google** — Google Workspace for Education
- **Microsoft** — Microsoft Azure AD / Entra ID

## What you need

Your district or school administrator must enable the SSO integration before users can sign in. If login fails, confirm with your admin that your account is rostered and that SSO is configured for your domain.

<Note>
  If SSO is not set up for your school, administrators can [resend login emails](/account/resend-login-emails) so users can sign in with a password link.
</Note>
