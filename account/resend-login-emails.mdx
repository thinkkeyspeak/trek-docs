If a student or staff member did not receive their welcome email—or cannot find it—an administrator can trigger a new login message from the admin dashboard.

## Resend a login email

1. Open the **Users** section of the admin dashboard.
2. Find the user who needs access.
3. Click the **pencil** icon next to their name.
4. Select **Reset Password**.

Trek sends a new login email to the address on file. Ask the user to check spam or junk folders if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

<Tip>
  Before resending, confirm the user's email address is correct in your roster or SIS. Typos are the most common cause of missing login messages.
</Tip>
