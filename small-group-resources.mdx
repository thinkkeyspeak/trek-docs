Small group instruction has its own rhythm — rotating stations, targeted mini-lessons, and independent work happening all at once. We're building dedicated guidance on **using TrekAi within small group models**, so teachers have practical ideas for where TrekAi fits (and where it doesn't) as students move through different stations.

_This resource is in development and will be added to the Ignite the Experience section of our support site soon. In the meantime, your Partner Success Manager can help you think through small group implementation for your classroom._
