title: "Students can't  login with Google or use Google Drive"
---

In Google Workspace for Education, students are blocked from third-party apps until the exact OAuth client used for Trek’s Google login is configured as Trusted for their organizational unit.

Here's how to fix it:

1. Go to Google Admin console → **Security → Access and data control → API controls → Manage Third-Party Access**.
2. Configure by **OAuth Client ID**. This is Trek's: [123576902546-m2fl21553v6hvlifkh1t10slg4d2fsht.apps.googleusercontent.com](http://123576902546-m2fl21553v6hvlifkh1t10slg4d2fsht.apps.googleusercontent.com)
3. Set **Trusted**, and apply to **student OUs** (or the whole organization).
