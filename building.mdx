---
title: "Digital Citizenship"
---

Good digital citizenship starts with a safe environment to practice it in, and that's built into TrekAi's design. TrekAi operates as a closed, secure digital ecosystem, with access strictly limited to your school community. By unifying tools and content into one controlled platform, the risks of the open web are eliminated, keeping students in a focused environment. The platform uses Socratic questioning to guide understanding rather than handing over answers, and teachers have full visibility into chat histories — so students are learning to think critically and use AI responsibly, with adult oversight built in rather than bolted on. 

On the data side, TrekAi's approach centers on transparency and safety first, limiting data collection to only what's required to deliver the service. Combined with FERPA/COPPA compliance and a 95% Common Sense Privacy rating, it gives schools a concrete, kid-safe example to point to when teaching students what responsible technology use looks like.
