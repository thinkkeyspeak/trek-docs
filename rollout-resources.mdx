TrekAi's team backs up every rollout with structure and support, not just a login link. Most districts complete full implementation within 4-6 weeks, covering technical integration, educator training, and a phased student rollout, with dedicated onboarding support and customized professional development for teachers and administrators along the way.

On the ground, a successful rollout typically starts with a planned Launch Day, when students receive their login emails and create accounts. Introducing TrekAi in a common class first lets students explore the platform, ask questions, and understand expectations for appropriate use, and assigning work through TrekAi early on helps reinforce adoption and build consistent habits. 

To support that plan, school and district leaders have access to:

- **Rollout & communication templates** — ready-made messaging for staff and families to introduce TrekAi ahead of launch
- **Teacher & Student Landing Pages** — role-specific hubs with training guides, slide decks, and shareable activities
- **Certification programs** — structured paths for both teachers and students to build confidence with the platform
- **Video resource libraries** — short walkthroughs for common tasks, organized by role
- **FAQs and Ideas from Educators** — practical tips sourced from other TrekAi schools

For anything not covered in these resources, a partner's onboarding lead or Partner Success Manager is the direct line for launch-day troubleshooting and ongoing questions.
