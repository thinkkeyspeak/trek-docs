**Rolling Out TrekAI: Keys to a Smooth Rollout** 

1. **Start with a clear why:** Tie the rollout to a real need (saving teacher time, closing instructional gaps).
2. **Phase it in:** Think through how you'll work towards school-wide adoption, with checkpoints to adjust.
3. **Train lightly, support well:** Decide what resources will best introduce TrekAi to your teachers, staff and students.

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="/videos/treklearning-student-overview.mp4" />

1. **Communicate with families:** Decide what resources will best introduce TrekAi to parents and families.
2. **Track a few simple metrics:** Think through how you will monitor daily active usage and resources generated and shared with students.
3. **Ask for feedback and act on it:** Plan how you will check in at 30, 60, 90 days and support teachers and students.
