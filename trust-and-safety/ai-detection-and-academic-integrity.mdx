Trek does **not** currently support AI-generated content detection. Detection tools often produce **false positives** that unfairly flag legitimate student work.

## What Trek does instead

Trek supports academic integrity through:

- **AI-resistant assignment design tools** — Prompts and structures that encourage original thinking
- **Teacher visibility** — Review **all student chats** for transparency and instructional oversight

## For teachers

Use assignment design and chat review together. Set clear classroom policies about when and how students may use Trek for each task.
