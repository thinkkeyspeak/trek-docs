Trek allows features to be **turned on or off** for specific students, grade levels, or instructional goals.

## Who configures access

**Administrators** work with the Trek team to customize which tools students can use. This is useful when:

- Younger grades need a simpler toolset
- A student needs accommodations
- Your district wants to phase in features over time

## Next steps

Contact your Trek administrator or Trek support to request changes to feature access for your school or district.
