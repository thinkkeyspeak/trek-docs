If sensitive information is accidentally entered into Trek, it can be **removed upon request**.

## Prevention

Trek shows clear warnings advising users **not to enter personal information** that is unnecessary for school work. Trek does not currently use automated detection for all sensitive data types.

## Who can access data

Trek runs in a secure environment where only **authorized teachers and administrators** can access student data. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Privacy evaluation

Trek is evaluated by **Common Sense Privacy**, with initial results placing the platform in the **top percentile** for privacy practices.

<Note>
  Contact your school administrator or Trek support to request removal of sensitive data. Do not post private student information in chat if it is not required for the assignment.
</Note>
