Trek is built for schools. Privacy and security are not optional add-ons—they are core to how the platform operates.

## Compliance

Trek is fully compliant with **COPPA** and **FERPA** requirements.

## Your school's data

- Data entered into Trek **belongs to the school**
- Trek **never sells, shares, or uses** student data to train external AI models
- Agreements with AI providers **prohibit** using your data for model training

## Access controls

Only **designated teachers and administrators** can access student data according to your school's permissions.
