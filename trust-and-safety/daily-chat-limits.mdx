Trek sets daily chat limits so usage stays predictable and sustainable for schools.

## Default limit

Each user has a default limit of **100 or more chats per day**.

## Need more?

If your school or district requires higher usage—for example during exam periods or large rollout weeks—the limit **can be increased**. Contact your administrator or Trek support to request an adjustment.
