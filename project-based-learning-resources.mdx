The TrekAi Teacher Certification gives educators hands-on training with TrekAi's powerful suite of tools. Through four dynamic modules, teachers learn how to streamline planning, save time on classroom tasks, and use AI to elevate student learning. By the end of the certification, teachers are equipped to integrate TrekAi confidently and effectively into their instruction.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Pinned Tools">
    - **Generate AI Slides** – Creates a downloadable slide deck from a topic and slide count.
    - **Assessment Generator** – Chat-based tool to build assessments from scratch or an uploaded resource.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Help with Assessments">
    - **Academic Differentiator** – Strategies and resources for diverse academic needs.
    - **AI-Resistant Assignment** – Assignment ideas that minimize reliance on AI-generated content.
    - **Assessment Modifier** – Adjusts existing assessments to fit new goals or needs.
    - **Blooms Labeler** – Labels assessment questions by Bloom's Taxonomy level.
    - **Math Word Problem** – Generates real-world math word problems.
    - **Question Generator from a Text** – Creates comprehension/analysis questions from any text.
    - **Standards Labeler** – Maps assessment questions to educational standards.
    - **Test Generator** – Generates quiz questions by topic and difficulty.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Plan Your Teaching">
    - **Essential Question** – Crafts inquiry-driving questions to anchor a unit or lesson.
    - **Guided Notes Generator** – Creates guided reading questions from a passage or article.
    - **Lab Report** – Templates for documenting scientific experiments.
    - **Lesson Planner** – Generates full lesson plans from topics, objectives, and standards.
    - **Project-Based Learning** – Designs student-centered PBL experiences.
    - **Rubrics** – Builds customizable rubrics for any assignment type.
    - **Sports Practice Planner** – Creates PE drills and practice plans.
    - **Sub Plan Generator** – Builds substitute lesson plans and worksheets.
    - **Worksheets** – Designs practice worksheets by subject and skill level.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Supporting Students">
    - **Book Recommender** – Suggests books by reading level, interest, and curriculum.
    - **Cultural Responsiveness** – Incorporates students' cultural backgrounds into lessons.
    - **Decodable Generator** – Produces phonics-based reading passages.
    - **IEP Generator** – Drafts individualized education plans with goals/accommodations.
    - **Social Emotional Responsiveness** – Integrates SEL into daily instruction.
    - **Text Leveler** – Adjusts reading materials to a target Lexile level.
    - **Vocabulary Stories** – Turns vocabulary lists into engaging stories.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Time Savers">
    - **Directions Polisher** – Rewrites instructions for clarity.
    - **Email Polisher** – Refines drafted emails for tone and clarity.
    - **Email Writer** – Composes professional school-related emails.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Other">
    - **Graphing** – Reads, interprets, and generates graphs for classroom use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
