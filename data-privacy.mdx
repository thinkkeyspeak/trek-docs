---
title: "Data Privacy"
---

Data privacy questions tend to top the list when a school considers a new AI tool, and TrekAi's answer is straightforward: TrekAi is fully compliant with COPPA and FERPA requirements. All data entered into TrekAi belongs to the school and is never sold, shared, or used to train external AI models. 

That protection extends to the AI itself: TrekAi does not use customer data to train other systems, and agreements with AI providers explicitly prohibit the use of data for model training. Access to data is restricted to designated teachers and administrators. 

In short — student and staff data stays where it belongs: with the school, visible only to the people who should see it, and never repurposed to train AI models elsewhere.
