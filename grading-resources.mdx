---
title: "Grading Resources"
---

Grading is one of the biggest time drains for teachers, and TrekAi is built to lighten that load. Teachers can generate lesson plans, build assessments, grade papers, and design slide decks in minutes instead of hours — TrekAi handles the administrative tasks that eat into evenings and weekends, giving teachers back an average of 6 hours per week. 

On the student side, when students upload written work into TrekAi, the platform provides detailed feedback to support revision, clarity, and academic growth — and students can upload an assignment rubric to get personalized feedback aligned with their teacher's grading criteria before ever submitting the final draft. 

Together, that means less time buried in red pen, and students arriving at final submissions already closer to the mark.
