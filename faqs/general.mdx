  <Accordion title="What is Trek Learning?">
    Trek Learning is an AI-powered learning platform designed specifically for K–12 schools. It provides students and teachers with a safe, structured environment to use artificial intelligence for learning, critical thinking, writing support, project-based learning, and skill development. Trek Learning is built with school-friendly safeguards, administrative controls, and curriculum alignment to support classroom instruction—not replace it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What problems does Trek Learning solve for schools?">
    Trek Learning helps schools address several key challenges:

    - **Safe AI Access:** Provides students with a monitored, education-focused AI platform instead of unrestricted public AI tools.
    - **Teacher Efficiency:** Supports teachers with instructional resources, feedback tools, and project-based learning ideas to reduce prep time.
    - **Student Skill Gaps:** Helps students strengthen writing, analysis, research, and critical thinking skills through guided AI interaction.
    - **Consistency & Oversight:** Gives administrators visibility into usage, ensuring AI is used responsibly and aligns with school policies.
    - **Equitable Access:** Ensures all students have structured access to AI tools, rather than only those who find external tools on their own.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Trek Learning available internationally?">
    Yes, we have teachers and students using the platform globally and work with partner schools from various countries.
  </Accordion>
