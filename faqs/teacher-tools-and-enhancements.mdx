---
title: "Teacher Tools & Enhancements"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What training is provided?">
    Our Partner Success Team offers both live training sessions and on-demand, pre-recorded trainings. You can choose the format that best fits your teachers' schedules and learning preferences.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Trek Learning offer tools for differentiated instruction?">
    Yes! Teachers can use:

    - **Essential Question Generator** for higher-order thinking.
    - **Lesson Plan Customization** to tailor instruction.
    - **Quiz Generator** with Depth of Knowledge (DOK) levels.
    - **AI-Resistant Assignments** to foster deeper thinking.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a tool for project-based learning (PBL)?">
    Yes! The PBL Tool generates project ideas, refines them, and creates lesson plans/rubrics for multidisciplinary learning.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there tools for AP test preparation?">
    While Trek Learning does not have AP-specific content, it supports AP students with:

    - **Quiz Generator** (AP-style questions)
    - **Essential Question Generator** (higher-order analysis)
    - **Tutor Mode** (study guide support)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upload my own materials?">
    Teachers can upload their own documents into Trek Learning. The platform allows educators to upload files like lesson materials, worksheets, readings, or PDFs so Trek Learning can reference that content when generating responses or helping learners.

    Admin can upload policies. See how [here](https://www.loom.com/share/4cf0c573c2f94e9aa4ac31109962f91f).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
