<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What LLM does Trek Learning use?">
    We use a variety of LLMs, including Claude, OpenAI, and GPT-4o, selecting the best model for each task. All data is stored in-house and not shared with third parties.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where does Trek Learning pull its information from?">
    TrekAi primarily uses accredited course content (115+ courses) and a general LLM knowledge base.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is Trek Learning's hallucination rate lower than ChatGPT?">
    Unlike ChatGPT, which pulls from the web, TrekAi is tied to a curated knowledge base, reducing misinformation to below 2%.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is Trek Learning different from other AI platforms?">
    TrekAi is a purpose-built educational AI that combines curriculum-aligned tutoring, teacher tools, enhanced accuracy, safety features, and analytics — which sets it apart from general-purpose AI platforms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Trek Learning offer any support for checking for plagiarism?">
    The reality is that no AI tool out there can say with complete certainty whether something was written by AI or plagiarized. Even the major detection platforms deal with accuracy issues and false positives. Because of that, we don't rely on a simple "AI or not" score.

    Instead, Trek focuses on giving teachers visibility into student growth over time — including writing patterns, skill development, and overall performance. That way, teachers can see how a student typically writes, how their thinking evolves, and whether a specific submission actually matches their demonstrated ability.

    So if a student uploads something created outside of Trek, teachers still have important context — not just the final submission, but a clear picture of that student's past work and progression.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can teachers and admin better see or preview the student interface?">
    At this time, teachers can preview student responses by toggling on student mode vs non-student mode. This short [**video**](https://www.loom.com/share/1cb86c734e0441beab76b9ac4c1a1cf6 "video") shows you how to toggle between modes.
  </Accordion>
