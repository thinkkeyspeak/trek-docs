---
title: "Student Onboarding & Best Practices"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the best practices for rolling out Trek Learning to students?">
    A good strategy is to introduce Trek Learning in a common class, allowing students to log in, explore, and ask questions before discussing appropriate use. Teachers assigning work through Trek Learning also helps with adoption.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the best practices for using Trek Learning in math?">
    Math teachers use Trek Learning as a homework companion or a flipped classroom tool. Recommended prompts include:

    - "Ask Trek to teach you about right triangles."
    - "Ask Trek to explain the Pythagorean theorem."
    - "Ask Trek to quiz you on the Pythagorean theorem."

    Students can also generate practice problems for self-quizzing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What grades is Trek Learning geared towards?">
    Trek Learning is most commonly used for grades 6-12, but some schools use it as early as 3rd grade. It also includes text-to-speech for accessibility.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which grades have the most success with Trek Learning?">
    Middle and High school students adapt quickly, but the platform benefits all grade levels.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
