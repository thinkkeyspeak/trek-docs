title: "Account Setup"
---

Once you sign up with TrekAi, you'll be connected with our Onboarding team, who will walk you through the entire process from start to finish. No need to reach out to find out what happens next! 

TrekAi offers a one-time integration upload with your sync source to onboard your teachers, students, and classes — powered by our integration partner, EdLink.

  See what integrations are already set up with TrekAi, and which sync sources are eligible (additional time required to set up a new integration).
</Card>

<Card title="EdLink Sharing Rules" icon="shield-check" href="https://ed.link/docs/platform/sharing/sharing-rules">
  Control exactly what roster data is shared with TrekAi through EdLink's sharing rules.
</Card>

If integration isn't an option, we also offer bulk upload templates and manual rostering, so every school has a path to getting set up.
