---
title: "Account Setup"
---

TrekAi offers three ways to get teachers and students set up, so schools can pick whatever fits their size and systems best:

- **EdLink integration** (recommended for larger rollouts) — connects directly to your SIS/LMS (Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology, Blackbaud, Veracross, FACTS/RenWeb, and more). Your system stays the source of truth, and TrekAi mirrors it, syncing automatically every few hours. Only one active EdLink connection is allowed per school.
- **CSV upload** — a good fit for schools without EdLink or with more custom rostering needs. Templates cover teachers, students, courses, and enrollments.
- **Manual setup** — best for smaller schools (under ~50 users), where admins add and adjust teachers, students, and courses directly in TrekAi at any time.

Whichever route a school takes, every teacher and student needs a **unique email address** to get an account provisioned.

Once accounts exist, logging in is simple: TrekAi supports Single Sign-On, currently integrating with both Google and Microsoft SSO, so students and staff can log in with the credentials they already use at school — no separate password to manage.
