---
title: "Differentiation Resources"
---

Meeting every student where they are is easier said than done, especially in a classroom with a wide range of readiness levels. TrekAi supports differentiated instruction through several teacher tools, including the Essential Question Generator for higher-order thinking, Lesson Plan Customization tools, Quiz Generators with Depth of Knowledge (DOK) levels, and AI-resistant assignment creation tools. 

On the student-facing side, the platform adapts to each student's comprehension level, learning style, pace, and accessibility requirements, with features like adjustable reading levels, multilingual support, and alternative explanation formats — so the same lesson can flex to support a struggling reader and stretch an advanced student, without requiring a teacher to build two separate versions of everything by hand.
