---
title: "AI and Hallucinations"
---

AI "hallucinations" — confidently wrong answers — are a real concern in the classroom, and TrekAi is built specifically to minimize them. TrekAi is built on a curriculum-aligned knowledge base and reports a hallucination rate of just 2%, compared to 15-20% for general-purpose tools like ChatGPT. That's because TrekAi's knowledge base is built specifically for K-12 education with secure content guardrails, so students get accurate, grade-appropriate guidance instead of open, unverified information from the internet. 

On top of the lower error rate, TrekAi uses Socratic teaching methods that guide students toward answers rather than simply providing them — so even in the rare case of an inaccurate response, students are building the habit of questioning and verifying rather than copying and moving on.
