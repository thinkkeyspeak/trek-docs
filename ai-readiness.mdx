**AI Readiness — Coming Soon**

Before rolling out TrekAi, many school leaders find it helpful to first understand where their staff and students already stand with AI — what tools they're already using, how comfortable teachers feel, and where the biggest questions or hesitations are.

We're building an **AI Readiness Survey and Prior AI Usage Inventory** to help with exactly that: a simple way to gauge your school's starting point before launch, so training and rollout can be tailored to where your community actually is, not just a one-size-fits-all plan.

_This resource is in development and will be added to the Orientation section of our support site soon. In the meantime, reach out to your Partner Success Manager if you'd like help thinking through your school's AI readiness ahead of launch._
